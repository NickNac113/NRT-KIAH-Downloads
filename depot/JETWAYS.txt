# X-Plane 12 native jetways

Terminal B already uses X-Plane 12's built-in animated jetways. There are 22
native jetway records at the rebuilt B North gates. They do not need a new
model, a second set of static bridges, or a conversion to use X-Plane control.

To let X-Plane control its default jetways while retaining OpenSAM for the
NRT aircraft-hangar doors:

1. Open the OpenSAM window and expand **Settings**.
2. Turn off **Manage XP12 Jetways**.
3. Fully quit and restart X-Plane. Reloading scenery alone is insufficient.
4. Use X-Plane's **Toggle jetway attachment** ground-handling button to dock
   or undock the native jetway at your parking position.

This is a global OpenSAM preference, not a per-airport choice. Keep OpenSAM
and openSAM_Library installed for the Main channel's hangar doors. The scenery
updater does not change this preference or replace any plugin files. Native
and OpenSAM docking can behave differently; check NRT9's dual-bridge operation
with the intended aircraft before relying on it. To return to OpenSAM jetway
management, enable the setting and restart X-Plane again.

## Why Terminal B disappeared in the camera view

The supplied log identifies OpenSAM 5.5.0-beta-3, commit
`d45807f748eff7dbfd0af14030ff853322f5e7dc`, with native-jetway management enabled.
That version creates native jetway instances inside a box extending 3,000 m
east/west and north/south of the **aircraft**, and removes instances outside it.
Moving the free camera does not move that box. B North's jetways are about
3,300-3,640 m west of the NRT aircraft position recorded in the log. The 65
native jetways inside the box match the log's instance count.

That is a strong explanation for this camera-only symptom, not a claim of
simulator acceptance. Other visible jetways may be static facades or use a
different implementation. Test with the aircraft at a B gate and, separately,
from NRT after turning off OpenSAM's native-jetway management. Do not duplicate
the jetways as a workaround. If B is still missing, retain the new session log
for further diagnosis.
