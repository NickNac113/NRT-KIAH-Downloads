refinement to the pre-campus airport and restores that area's original ground,
starts and vehicle routes. See VARIANT.txt for this copy's contents.

Both choices share version 0.28.0 and Custom Scenery/NRT_KIAH_Campus. Use
SkunkCrafts 4.2.3 or newer, check for the channel offer, select a channel and run
a full check and repair/update with X-Plane closed. Restart after switching.

VERIFY IN X-PLANE
Check Terminal B glazing/passenger artwork by day and night, fixed bridge joins,
animated jetway docking, ND/R1/NG guidance and service movement. For Main also
recheck the outstanding campus items in INSTALL.txt. Airport Only must contain
