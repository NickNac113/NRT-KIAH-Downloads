# Install NRT KIAH Campus

Download the latest scenery ZIP from [NRT KIAH Downloads](https://nicknac113.github.io/NRT-KIAH-Downloads/).

1. Close X-Plane. Extract the ZIP into your X-Plane 12 folder. The result must be `Custom Scenery/NRT_KIAH_Campus/skunkcrafts_updater.cfg`, with `Earth nav data` beside it. Avoid a second nested `Custom Scenery` folder.
2. Keep **MisterX Library**, **OpenSAM**, and **openSAM_Library** installed. These are separate dependencies and are not included in this download. Use their developer distributions; keep the copies already working on your PC. **AEP is not required.**
3. Back up `Custom Scenery/scenery_packs.ini`. Disable previous NRT/KIAH test scenery entries (R20 through R27, or any other versioned NRT airport) so only this KIAH package is active. Keep their folders for rollback. Put `SCENERY_PACK Custom Scenery/NRT_KIAH_Campus/` above other KIAH airport packages and Global Airports, with the libraries below the airport. Keep the final slash.
4. Run the current [SkunkCrafts standalone updater](https://updater.skunkcrafts.com/) against this X-Plane folder. It should find **NRT KIAH Campus**. Check and update when offered, with X-Plane closed.
5. Fully restart X-Plane after updating, then load KIAH. If controls or jetways are missing, run `Check OpenSAM.cmd` in the scenery folder and enter the X-Plane root. It reads the scenery configuration and Log.txt without changing them.

The current member release is **0.27.0 (R27)**, including R25-R27 changes. Follow the yellow canopy entry and forward-exit lines back to the main taxi line. G650 and Lineage are the turning targets; BBJ2 requires tow-in/tow-out. The canopy and NRT11 approach share space: one aircraft movement at a time.

NRT11 parks nose east. Its aircraft planning limits are **66 m / 216.5 ft wingspan AND 50 m / 164 ft length**; both limits apply. Push back or tow out. Its walk approaches from the north and continues to the hold point outside the single NRT10 helipad. Use the walk with aircraft stationary and rotors stopped. Ramp fencing separates the aircraft area from public parking.

Hangars are configured open by default. Six OpenSAM controls remain: MH1 Outer/Middle/Tail, MH2, and Indoor Parking Left/Right. Truck workshop doors are fixed open. Indoor Parking has a 20.6 m main roof and 16 m clear opening. Both headquarters signs read NRT VIRTUAL HEADQUARTERS. All 119 native airport/FBO jetway records remain; both NRT9 jetways and their fixed links now match the stand's 4 m eastward move.

R27 adds local service-vehicle parking and road access, a stock mast beside NRT9/NRT11, and modeled sheltered floors under the canopy and Indoor Parking. Exterior native wet surfaces remain. Recheck NB service routes, both NRT9 jetways, sheltered floors in rain, and night lighting in a fresh session. These are offline-verified changes; simulator behavior and appearance are not yet accepted. Native service AI can leave roads on its final approach. See `README_R27.txt` for the test sequence.

Future updates replace files inside the same `NRT_KIAH_Campus` folder. They do not manage libraries, plugins, aircraft, preferences, or `scenery_packs.ini`. Keep personal custom files outside managed paths or use SkunkCrafts' ignore file. Old versioned scenery folders are not removed automatically.

For rollback, close X-Plane, disable the stable folder and re-enable your previous versioned package. Download links are public; a GitHub account is not required.
