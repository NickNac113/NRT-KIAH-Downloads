# Install NRT KIAH Campus

Download the latest scenery ZIP from [NRT KIAH Downloads](https://nicknac113.github.io/NRT-KIAH-Downloads/).

1. Close X-Plane. Extract the ZIP into your X-Plane 12 folder. The result must be `Custom Scenery/NRT_KIAH_Campus/skunkcrafts_updater.cfg`, with `Earth nav data` beside it. Avoid a second nested `Custom Scenery` folder.
2. Keep **MisterX Library**, **OpenSAM**, and **openSAM_Library** installed. These are separate dependencies and are not included in this download. Use their developer distributions; keep the copies already working on your PC.
3. Back up `Custom Scenery/scenery_packs.ini`. Disable previous NRT/KIAH test scenery entries (R20, R21, R22, R23, R24, etc.) so only this KIAH package is active. Keep their folders for rollback. Put `SCENERY_PACK Custom Scenery/NRT_KIAH_Campus/` above other KIAH airport packages and Global Airports, with the libraries below the airport. Keep the final slash.
4. Run the current [SkunkCrafts standalone updater](https://updater.skunkcrafts.com/) against this X-Plane folder. It should find **NRT KIAH Campus**. Check and update when offered, with X-Plane closed.
5. Fully exit and restart X-Plane after updating, then load KIAH. Check hangar doors, jetways, canopy clearance, pavement and night lighting on your PC. If controls or jetways are missing, run `Check OpenSAM.cmd` in the scenery folder and enter the X-Plane root. It reads the scenery configuration and Log.txt without changing them.

The current member release is **0.24.0 (R24)**. Follow the new yellow canopy entry and forward-exit lines back to the main taxi line. The G650 and Lineage are the turning targets; BBJ2 operations still require tow-in/tow-out. The canopy stop and passenger doorway move 11 m north. NRT11 parks nose east, with a planning limit of 20 m wingspan and 22 m length; push back or tow out. Its marked walk approaches from the north and continues to the hold point outside the single NRT10 helipad. Use the walk with aircraft stationary and rotors stopped.

Hangars are configured open by default. Six OpenSAM controls remain: MH1 outer/middle/tail, MH2, and Indoor Parking Left/Right. Truck workshop doors are fixed open. The renamed Indoor Parking hangar has a 20.6 m main roof and 16 m clear opening. Both headquarters signs read NRT VIRTUAL HEADQUARTERS. All 119 native airport/FBO jetway records and existing service routes are retained. Live door operation, taxi clearance, jetways, marshalling, vehicle motion, wet surfaces and lighting still require testing on your PC. See `README_R24.txt` for test notes.

The next-revision security fence around the aircraft ramp, separating it from public parking, is queued separately and is not included in R24. The current release corrects the cargo blast fences and removes the obsolete arrival-court fence and red parking marks.

Future updates replace files inside the same `NRT_KIAH_Campus` folder. They do not manage libraries, plugins, aircraft, preferences, or `scenery_packs.ini`. Keep personal custom files outside managed paths or use SkunkCrafts' ignore file. Old versioned scenery folders are not removed automatically.

For rollback, close X-Plane, disable the stable folder and re-enable your previous versioned package. Download links are public; a GitHub account is not required.
